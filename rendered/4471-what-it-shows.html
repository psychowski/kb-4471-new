#4471 · two problems, not one

owner psychowski living reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

#4471 was merged as one issue (“dates parse mm/dd in eu locales”). The 412 reports in it are two different problems. Each needs its own fix and its own count.

"One issue, one fix queued" is wrong. Fixing the parser closes the biggest problem and leaves the other one open.

The problems

5 of the 379 parser reports read as a one-day move. They are filed and counted under parser by tenant.

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants who write the day first see the month flipped. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it their way. The parser does not cause this. A parser fix will not touch it.

Five parser reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) read as a one-day move, not a day and month swapped. The desk files them under their tenant’s cause, so they are counted in parser.

How the problems were told apart

Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. That tag is a guess: it says parser for 15 of the 33 hint reports and hint for 23 of the 379 parser reports. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and which booking form it runs, so the counts above come from the tenant directory and the channel, never from the tag.

Why #4471 hid this

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. A subject line does not say which bug a report is about, so two bugs ended up in one ticket. The count of 412 is right. The single ticket is wrong.

What to do

  • Keep one ticket per problem and count its reports separately.
  • Check a report’s tenant and booking form before you merge it into a ticket.
  • Re-read this page against the live ticket views before you rely on it.
